Features

Everything a private practice with a pharmacy needs

Built for a single clinic or small practice where one or more doctors work alongside a front desk and an in-house dispensary.

Patients & families

Register patients, group them into families, spot possible duplicates, and see each patient's full visit history.

Appointments & queue

Book appointments or add walk-ins. A live queue moves patients from waiting to called to consulting.

Consultations

Record vitals, clinical notes and diagnoses. Allergies and chronic conditions are flagged on screen.

Prescriptions

Look medicines up by name or generic, get allergy warnings, repeat earlier prescriptions and print them.

Pharmacy dispensing

Prescriptions reach the pharmacy instantly. Pick, check and hand over each item, including partial dispensing.

Inventory & expiry

Batch and expiry tracking, low-stock and expiry alerts, stock counts and reason-coded adjustments.

Suppliers & purchasing

Keep a supplier directory, raise purchase orders and record goods received against them.

Billing & payments

One invoice covers the consultation and medicines. Cash, card or mobile, split payments, discounts and day-end reconciliation.

Reports & dashboards

Patient volume, revenue, top medicines, follow-ups due and stock alerts, with PDF and Excel export.

How it works

A patient's visit, start to finish

  1. Step 1

    Reception

    The patient is registered or found, then booked or added to the queue.

  2. Step 2

    Consultation

    The doctor calls the next patient, records vitals and notes, and writes the prescription.

  3. Step 3

    Pharmacy

    The prescription appears at the pharmacy right away. Medicines are picked from the right batch.

  4. Step 4

    Payment

    One invoice for the visit and medicines. The patient pays and leaves with a receipt.

Security & reliability

Your patient data stays in your clinic

E-POCH runs on a computer inside your clinic and is reached over your local network. Patient records are not sent to an outside server, and the system keeps working when the internet is down.

  • Role-based access. Doctors, reception and pharmacy only see and do what their role allows.
  • Audit trail. Changes to clinical and billing records are logged with the user and time.
  • Encrypted backups. Scheduled backups to an external drive, with a tested restore process.
  • Simple setup. A typical clinic needs one front-desk computer as the server. Other computers only need a browser.

FAQ

Common questions

What is E-POCH Medical System?

It is clinic management software for private practices with their own pharmacy. It handles patients, appointments, consultations, prescriptions, dispensing, stock and billing in one place.

Do we need an internet connection?

No. The system runs on your clinic's local network. Internet is only needed for updates or off-site backups if you choose them.

What computers do we need?

The usual setup is two computers: one at the front desk that also runs the system, and one for the doctor. Any modern browser works on the other computers.

Can one person handle both reception and pharmacy?

Yes. A front-desk account covers both, and staff switch between the two screens without signing out.

Can we move our existing patient records?

Talk to us about what you use today. We'll tell you what can be brought across before you decide.

Do you help with installation and training?

Yes. We install the system at your clinic, set up backups and train your staff. Contact us to arrange a visit.
